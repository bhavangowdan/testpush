## Question
6. Your `TRADECONF_TBS` tablespace is created with `AUTOEXTEND ON NEXT 32M MAXSIZE 2G`. What does this mean operationally?:

## Options
Option 1: Oracle creates a new datafile whenever storage grows by 32 MiB, with a total limit of 2 GiB per tablespace

Option 2: The current datafile autoextends by 32 MiB when full, up to 2 GiB; beyond that, another file is needed

Option 3: Oracle allocates the full 2 GiB during creation and internally manages storage in fixed 32 MiB chunks

Option 4: The 32 MiB and 2 GiB values define SGA memory allocation limits rather than datafile growth settings

## Answers
Option 2 : 1
